How to filter

Type in the box under any column heading. Filters on different columns combine.

>10 <=3.5greater than, at most
10..50between 10 and 50, inclusive
=4 !=4equal to, not equal to
tau !tautext contains, does not contain
* !*has a value, is missing

Click a heading to sort; click again to reverse. Shift-click another heading to add a secondary sort. Missing values always sort last.

Your filters and selection carry over to the Plots page.
